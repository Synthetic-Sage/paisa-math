Why Goal Planning Requires Inflation Adjustment

Most beginner investors make the mistake of aiming for the current cost of their financial goal. If a child's higher education costs ₹20 Lakhs today, saving exactly ₹20 Lakhs over 15 years will result in a massive shortfall, because the cost of education increases every year.

How The Calculator Works

Our algorithm performs two distinct calculations instantly:

  1. Inflation Projection: We take your target amount and project its future cost using the expected inflation rate (Future Value = Present Value × (1 + inflation)^years).
  2. Reverse SIP Calculation: Once the true future target is known, we use the Future Value of Annuity formula in reverse to solve for the exact monthly SIP required to hit that inflated target, based on your expected return rate.
