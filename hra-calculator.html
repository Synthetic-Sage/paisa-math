HRA Calculation Rules in India

House Rent Allowance (HRA) is a major component of an employee's salary package. The Income Tax Act allows a deduction for HRA under Section 10(13A), but the entire amount received is rarely tax-free.

The Rule of Minimum

The tax-exempt portion of your HRA is the lowest of the following three amounts:

  1. 1. The actual HRA amount received from your employer.
  2. 2. Actual rent paid minus 10% of your Basic Salary + DA.
  3. 3. 50% of your Basic Salary + DA (if you live in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, or Chennai) OR 40% (for all other cities).

Note: HRA exemption is only applicable if you opt for the Old Tax Regime. The New Tax Regime does not allow HRA exemptions.
