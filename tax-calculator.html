Old vs New Tax Regime: Explained

The Indian Government has pushed the New Tax Regime as the default option for FY 2025-26. It offers significantly lower tax rates and an increased standard deduction, but forces you to let go of almost all traditional tax exemptions (like 80C, HRA, LTA).

New Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025-26)

  • Nil (0%) Up to ₹3,00,000
  • 5% ₹3,00,001 - ₹7,00,000
  • 10% ₹7,00,001 - ₹10,00,000
  • 15% ₹10,00,001 - ₹12,00,000
  • 20% ₹12,00,001 - ₹15,00,000
  • 30% Above ₹15,00,000

Note on Rebate 87A

Under the New Regime, if your taxable income is up to ₹7,00,000 (effectively ₹7.75 Lakhs for salaried individuals after the ₹75,000 standard deduction), your tax liability is reduced to ZERO.
