The Power of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP)

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a facility offered by mutual funds to investors, allowing them to invest small amounts periodically (usually monthly) instead of a lump sum. In India, SIPs have become the most popular wealth-creation tool because they instill financial discipline and mitigate market volatility through Rupee Cost Averaging.

How Does the SIP Calculator Work?

Our SIP calculator uses the future value of annuity formula to project your potential wealth over time. Mutual fund investments compound over the years, meaning you earn returns not just on your principal, but also on the accumulated returns of previous years. This compounding effect creates the "magic" of long-term wealth creation.

Understanding the Results

When you calculate your SIP, our visual Doughnut Chart splits your final wealth into two categories:

  • Invested Amount (Blue): The actual cash you deposited over the entire tenure.
  • Estimated Returns (Green): The pure profit generated by compound interest. Notice how over long periods (15+ years), the green portion massively overtakes your invested amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?
No. SIPs in mutual funds are subject to market risks. The expected return rate is an estimate based on historical data. Equity mutual funds historically deliver around 10-12% over the long term (7+ years), but it is not a fixed guarantee.
Can I pause or stop my SIP at any time?
Yes, one of the biggest advantages of an SIP is flexibility. You can stop, pause, or increase your SIP amount at any time without paying any penalty to the Asset Management Company (AMC).
What is Rupee Cost Averaging?
Rupee Cost Averaging is an investment strategy where you buy more units when the market (NAV) is low and fewer units when the market is high. By investing a fixed amount every month via SIP, you naturally average out your purchase cost over time.
