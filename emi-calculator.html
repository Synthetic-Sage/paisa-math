Mastering Your Loan EMI in India

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are utilized to pay off both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is fully paid off. In India, EMIs are the backbone of repaying home loans, car loans, and personal loans from major institutions like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank.

How is EMI Calculated? The Formula

E = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n - 1)
  • E is the EMI amount you will pay every month.
  • P is the Principal Loan Amount.
  • r is the monthly interest rate (calculated as Annual Rate / 12 / 100).
  • n is the loan tenure in months (Years × 12).

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reduce my EMI amount?
To reduce your monthly EMI, you can do three things: negotiate a lower interest rate with your bank, make a larger down payment to reduce the principal loan amount, or increase the tenure of the loan (e.g., from 15 years to 20 years). Note that increasing tenure reduces EMI but increases total interest paid.
Is the EMI formula the same for all Indian banks?
Yes. Whether you are taking a loan from SBI, HDFC, ICICI, or a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), the mathematical formula used to calculate EMI on reducing balance loans is universally the same.
