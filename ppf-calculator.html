Why PPF is India's Favorite Safe Investment

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a savings-cum-tax-saving instrument in India introduced by the National Savings Institute. It offers an attractive rate of interest and returns that are fully exempted from Income Tax under Section 80C.

The Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) Benefit

PPF is one of the very few financial instruments in India that falls under the prestigious "EEE" tax category:

  • Exempt 1 (Investment): Your annual investment amount (up to ₹1.5 Lakhs) is tax-deductible under Section 80C.
  • Exempt 2 (Accumulation): The interest earned every year on your PPF balance is completely tax-free.
  • Exempt 3 (Withdrawal): The final maturity amount you withdraw after 15 years is entirely tax-free.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lock-in period for PPF?
The standard lock-in period is 15 years. You cannot close the account before this period. However, partial withdrawals are allowed from the 7th financial year onwards.
