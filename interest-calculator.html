Simple vs. Compound Interest

Understanding the difference between simple and compound interest is the foundation of finance. While simple interest is easy to calculate, compound interest is the engine of true wealth creation.

Simple Interest Formula

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

With simple interest, you only earn interest on your original principal. If you invest ₹100 at 10% for 3 years, you earn ₹10 every year, totaling ₹30 in interest.

Compound Interest Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)nt

With compound interest, you earn interest on your principal AND on your accumulated interest. Using the same ₹100 at 10%:

  • Year 1: ₹10 interest (Total ₹110)
  • Year 2: 10% of ₹110 = ₹11 interest (Total ₹121)
  • Year 3: 10% of ₹121 = ₹12.1 interest (Total ₹133.1)
