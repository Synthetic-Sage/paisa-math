How Fixed Deposits Work in India

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a financial instrument provided by banks or NBFCs which provides investors a higher rate of interest than a regular savings account, until the given maturity date.

Quarterly Compounding Explained

Most major Indian banks, including SBI, HDFC, and ICICI, compute interest on a quarterly compounding basis. This means the interest earned in the first quarter is added to your principal, and in the next quarter, you earn interest on both your original principal and the previous quarter's interest. The formula used is:

A = P × (1 + r/n)n×t
  • A: Maturity Amount
  • P: Principal Amount
  • r: Annual interest rate (decimal)
  • n: Compounding frequency (4 times a year for Indian banks)
  • t: Tenure in years
