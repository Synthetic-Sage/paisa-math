Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data Security & Local Processing

PaisaMath is committed to your privacy. We are a collection of static financial calculators. This means we do not collect, store, or transmit your financial data (such as your loan amounts, interest rates, or SIP contributions) to any servers. All mathematics and processing happen securely and instantly within your own web browser.

2. Advertising and Cookies

We rely on third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements that keep our calculators 100% free for everyone in India. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.

3. Analytics

We may use standard analytics tools to see which calculators are the most popular (e.g., whether more people are using the EMI or SIP calculator today). This data is completely anonymous and cannot be tied back to you or your specific financial numbers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any queries regarding our privacy practices or the math behind our calculators, please email us at privacy@paisamath.in.
