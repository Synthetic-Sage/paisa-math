Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

At PaisaMath, your privacy is our highest priority. We do not collect, store, or transmit any financial data you enter into our calculators.

All calculations are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript. When you close the tab, the data disappears. We have no servers capturing your salary, investments, or targets.

2. Analytics and Cookies

We may use basic analytics (like Google Analytics or Vercel Analytics) to understand how many people visit the site and which calculators are most popular. This data is entirely anonymous and aggregated. We do not use advertising cookies to track you across the web.

3. Local Storage

In the future, we may offer features to save your preferences (like a preferred tax regime or color theme) using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Links

Our resources section may contain links to third-party financial institutions or blogs. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites once you leave PaisaMath.

TL;DR

We are a static website. We don't want your data, we don't store your data, and we can't see your data.
