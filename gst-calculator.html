Understanding GST Calculation in India

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax used in India on the supply of goods and services. It is a comprehensive, multistage, destination-based tax that has replaced many indirect taxes in India.

Exclusive vs Inclusive GST

  • Exclusive GST (+): The product price does not include GST. Tax is calculated on top of the base price.
    Formula: GST Amount = (Base Price × GST Rate) / 100
  • Inclusive GST (-): The product price already includes the tax. You calculate backwards to find the base price and the tax component.
    Formula: GST Amount = Total Price - [Total Price × (100 / (100 + GST Rate))]

CGST, SGST, and IGST Explained

For intra-state transactions (sales within the same state), the total GST rate is divided equally between the Central Government (CGST) and the State Government (SGST). For example, an 18% GST becomes 9% CGST and 9% SGST. For inter-state transactions, the entire tax goes to the central government as IGST.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard GST slabs in India?
Goods and services are divided into five different tax slabs for collection of tax: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Essential items usually fall under 0% or 5%, while luxury items are taxed at 28%.
